Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use Cell Cave applications and related services, including information that may be processed by third-party advertising and service providers.

1. Overview

Cell Cave develops practical utility applications designed to help with everyday digital tasks.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be processed when you use our apps, why that information may be used, and the role of third-party services integrated into our applications.

2. Information That May Be Processed

The information processed while using a Cell Cave app can depend on the app, the features you use, your device settings, and the third-party services included in that app.

Information you provide

If you contact Cell Cave for support, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide, such as your email address, the contents of your message, and any information you choose to include with your support request.

Device and app information

Third-party services used by an app may automatically process technical information. Depending on the service and your settings, this may include information such as:

  • Device type and operating system information
  • App version and technical identifiers
  • IP address or approximate location derived from it
  • Advertising identifiers where permitted
  • App interactions and advertisement interactions
  • Diagnostic or performance information

The exact information handled by a third-party provider is governed by that provider's own privacy policy and applicable permissions, settings, and consent requirements.

3. How Information May Be Used

Depending on the app and services being used, information may be processed for purposes such as:

  • Providing and maintaining app functionality
  • Responding to support requests
  • Improving stability and technical performance
  • Displaying and measuring advertising
  • Preventing fraud or invalid advertising activity
  • Meeting applicable legal or platform requirements

4. Advertising and Monetization

Some Cell Cave apps may use third-party advertising services to display, deliver, measure, and manage ads.

Depending on the advertising provider, your region, consent choices, and device settings, these services may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, app activity, ad interactions, and related technical information.

Advertising providers may use information according to their own policies and the choices available through your device, operating system, consent interface, or advertising settings.

6. Data Sharing

Information may be processed or shared with third-party service providers where necessary to provide their advertising, technical, measurement, fraud-prevention, or related services.

Third-party providers handle information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements. Cell Cave may also disclose information where required to comply with applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

7. Data Retention

Information sent directly to Cell Cave, such as a support email, may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, maintain appropriate records, resolve issues, or meet applicable obligations.

Information processed independently by third-party services is retained according to the policies and retention practices of those providers.

8. Security

Cell Cave takes reasonable care when handling information received directly through support communications and when selecting services used within its applications.

However, no method of electronic transmission, processing, or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Cell Cave does not knowingly request children to provide personal information directly to Cell Cave through support communications.

Third-party advertising services may have their own rules, age requirements, and privacy practices. Their applicable policies should be reviewed for additional information.

10. External Links

Cell Cave apps and websites may contain links to external websites or services. Cell Cave does not control the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external service you visit.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised when our apps, third-party services, legal requirements, or privacy practices change.

When this policy is updated, the date shown at the top of this page may also be updated.
