Privacy Policy for Status Downloader: Video Saver

This Privacy Policy explains how Cell Cave handles information when you use Status Downloader: Video Saver, including local media access, analytics, advertising, subscriptions, payments, and third-party services.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

1. Overview

Status Downloader: Video Saver is provided by Cell Cave.

The app is designed to help users save and manage supported status and media content on their device.

The app does not require users to create a Cell Cave account or sign in with a name, email address, phone number, or other personal account information.

2. Information We Handle

Information we do not directly request

The app does not require you to provide the following information in order to use its main features:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • A Cell Cave account or password
  • Precise GPS location
  • Credit or debit card numbers directly to Cell Cave

Technical and usage information

The app uses third-party analytics and advertising technologies. Depending on the service, your device settings, your region, and the configuration of those services, these providers may automatically process technical information such as:

  • Device and app information
  • Operating system information
  • App interactions and usage events
  • Advertising or app identifiers
  • IP address and network information
  • Advertising interactions
  • Diagnostic or performance information
  • Approximate location that may be inferred from information such as an IP address
Precise Location

Status Downloader: Video Saver does not request precise GPS location for its core functionality. Some third-party providers may infer a general or approximate location from technical information such as an IP address.

Contacting support

If you voluntarily contact Cell Cave by email, we receive the email address you use, the contents of your message, and any information you choose to include so that we can respond to your request.

3. Photos, Videos, Media and Storage Access

Status Downloader: Video Saver may request access to photos, videos, media, or device storage when this access is necessary to provide its supported media-saving and management features.

Media accessed through these permissions is handled locally on your device for the app's functionality.

Cell Cave does not upload your saved status photos or videos to a Cell Cave server as part of the app's normal functionality.

You can manage or revoke media permissions through your Android device settings. If required access is disabled, some media-related features may not function correctly.

4. Firebase Analytics

Status Downloader: Video Saver uses Firebase Analytics, a Google service, to better understand how the app is used and to help evaluate app performance and user interactions.

Firebase Analytics is used for both free users and paid subscribers.

Depending on Google's service configuration, Firebase may process app usage events, device or app information, identifiers, technical information, and related analytics data.

Firebase and Google process information according to their own terms and privacy practices.

8. Data Storage and Retention

Status photos, videos, and supported media handled by the app remain on your device and are not stored on Cell Cave servers as part of the app's normal functionality.

You control locally stored media through your device and may delete it using the app's supported functions or your device's storage and media tools.

Analytics, advertising, payment, and other information processed by third-party providers may be retained according to each provider's own retention policies and applicable legal requirements.

If you contact us for support, we may retain your support communication for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, maintain support records, or meet applicable obligations.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your device, location, and applicable law, you may have choices regarding permissions, analytics, advertising, and personal information.

  • You can manage Photos, Videos, Media, or Storage permissions through Android settings.
  • You can manage relevant advertising and privacy choices through your device and any consent controls presented by applicable advertising providers.
  • You can purchase the paid subscription to use the app without advertisements.
  • You can manage your subscription through Google Play.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time.
  • You may contact Cell Cave regarding applicable privacy questions or requests.

Privacy rights may differ depending on your country or region.

10. Children's Privacy

Status Downloader: Video Saver is not directed to children under the age of 13.

Cell Cave does not knowingly require children under 13 to create an account or directly provide personal contact information through the app.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child under 13 has directly provided personal information to Cell Cave, please contact us so that we can review the request and take appropriate action where required.

11. Data Security

The app's supported status photos and videos are handled locally on the user's device rather than being uploaded to a Cell Cave server as part of normal functionality.

Information processed by Firebase, advertising networks, Google Play, or other third-party providers is subject to the security practices and policies of those providers.

No electronic transmission, device, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. International Data Processing

Third-party providers used by the app may operate in multiple countries. As a result, technical, analytics, advertising, or payment information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live.

Such processing is governed by the applicable provider's privacy policy and relevant legal requirements.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Cell Cave may update this Privacy Policy when app functionality, third-party services, privacy practices, or applicable requirements change.

When this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page will be revised.

We encourage users to review this page periodically for the latest information about the app's privacy practices.
