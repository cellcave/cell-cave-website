Privacy Policy for Cloud Backup: Photo Storage

This Privacy Policy explains how Cell Cave handles information when you use Cloud Backup: Photo Storage, including account registration, encrypted cloud backups, permissions, restore functionality, analytics, advertising, subscriptions, payments, and the in-app assistant.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Contents

1. Overview

Cloud Backup: Photo Storage is provided by Cell Cave.

The app allows users to create an account, back up supported content to cloud storage, and restore supported content when needed.

Supported backup content may include:

  • Photos
  • Videos
  • Documents
  • Contacts

Backup files are encrypted on the user's device before they leave the device for cloud storage.

2. Account Registration and OTP Verification

Cloud Backup: Photo Storage requires users to create an account using an email address.

The email address is used for account registration, sign-in, account verification, and related account functionality.

An OTP, or one-time password, may be sent to the user's email address to verify access to the account.

Account and authentication services may be provided using Amazon Web Services infrastructure.

Information associated with account access

Depending on the authentication process, information handled may include:

  • Email address
  • Account identifier
  • Authentication and verification information
  • Sign-in events and related technical data

OTP codes are used for account verification and security purposes.

3. Encrypted Cloud Backup and Restore

Cloud Backup: Photo Storage allows users to back up supported photos, videos, documents, and contacts.

Backup files are encrypted on the user's device before they are transferred for cloud storage.

Encrypted backup files are stored using Amazon Web Services (AWS) cloud infrastructure.

Cell Cave Cannot Access Backup File Contents

Backup files are protected before they leave the user's device, and Cell Cave cannot access the contents of the user's encrypted backup files.

Restore functionality

The app may allow users to restore supported backed-up content to their device.

Restore functionality requires the app to retrieve the user's encrypted backup data and make it available to the authenticated user.

Backup and restore operations may require an internet connection.

4. Device Permissions

Cloud Backup: Photo Storage may request device permissions when needed to provide backup and restore functionality.

Photos and Media Access

The app may request access to photos, videos, and other supported media so that users can select content for backup or restore.

Where supported by Android, users may be provided with options such as:

  • Full Access
  • Limited Access
  • No Access

The app uses the media access granted by the user through the operating system's permission controls.

Contacts Access

The app may request Contacts permission when the user chooses to back up or restore contacts.

Contacts access is used for supported contact backup and restore functionality.

Users can manage or revoke permissions through Android device settings.

If a required permission is denied, the related backup or restore feature may not be available.

5. In-App Assistant

Cloud Backup: Photo Storage may include an assistant feature that allows users to type questions and receive generated replies.

When a user chooses to use the assistant, the text entered into the assistant must be processed in order to generate a response.

The assistant functionality uses services provided through Amazon Web Services (AWS) .

Information entered into the assistant may therefore be transmitted to and processed by AWS-supported services for the purpose of generating the requested response.

Do Not Enter Sensitive Information

Users should avoid entering passwords, payment card details, OTP codes, or other highly sensitive information into the assistant.

6. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Cloud Backup: Photo Storage may use Google Play services and Firebase technologies for app analytics, crash reporting, performance monitoring, and related app functionality.

Depending on the specific Firebase and Google services configured in the app, these services may process information such as:

  • Device and app information
  • Operating system information
  • App usage and interaction events
  • App or device identifiers
  • Crash logs
  • Diagnostic information
  • Performance information
  • IP address and network information

These services process information according to Google's own privacy practices.

Cloud Backup: Photo Storage may display advertisements using Google AdMob .

AdMob may process advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, advertising interactions, network information, and related technical information according to Google's privacy practices.

In regions where consent is required, Google or the app may present privacy or consent controls relating to advertising.

Advertising availability may depend on the user's plan, region, consent choices, and current app configuration.

8. Paid Plans, Subscriptions and Purchases

Cloud Backup: Photo Storage offers paid plans.

Subscriptions and supported in-app purchases are processed using Google Play Billing .

Cell Cave does not directly receive or process the user's full credit or debit card number for purchases processed through Google Play.

Google may process payment, billing, transaction, account, and related information according to Google's own policies.

Subscription management, renewal, cancellation, and available payment methods are handled through the user's Google Play account.

10. Data Storage and Retention

Encrypted backup files

Encrypted backup files may remain stored in AWS cloud infrastructure while they are associated with the user's account and backup service.

Cell Cave cannot access the contents of the user's encrypted backup files.

Account information

Email address, account identifiers, authentication information, and related account records may be retained as necessary to provide account and backup services.

Analytics and crash information

Analytics, diagnostics, crash data, advertising information, and payment information handled by third-party providers may be retained according to those providers' own retention policies.

Support communications

If you contact Cell Cave for support, we may retain your communication for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, maintain support records, or meet applicable obligations.

11. Your Privacy Choices

  • You can choose Full Access, Limited Access, or No Access for supported media permissions where Android provides those choices.
  • You can manage Photos, Videos, Media, Contacts, and other applicable permissions through Android device settings.
  • You can choose which supported files or data you want to back up.
  • You can manage applicable advertising and privacy consent choices through available app or device controls.
  • You can manage paid subscriptions and purchases through Google Play.
  • You can choose whether to use the in-app assistant.
  • You can contact Cell Cave regarding applicable privacy or account requests.

Privacy rights may vary depending on the user's country or region.

12. Children's Privacy

Cloud Backup: Photo Storage is not directed to children under the age of 13.

Cell Cave does not knowingly design this app specifically for children under 13.

If a parent or guardian believes a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, please contact Cell Cave so the request can be reviewed.

13. Security and Encryption

Cloud Backup: Photo Storage is designed so that supported backup files are encrypted on the user's device before the files leave the device for cloud storage.

Cell Cave cannot access the contents of the user's encrypted backup files.

AWS infrastructure is used for cloud storage and related services.

Users should keep their email account, device, OTP codes, and account credentials secure.

No network, device, encryption system, or electronic storage service can be guaranteed to be completely secure in all circumstances.

14. International Data Processing

AWS, Google, and other infrastructure used to provide the app may operate data centers and services in multiple countries.

Account information, encrypted backup data, analytics information, crash reports, advertising information, payment information, and assistant requests may therefore be processed in countries other than the country where the user lives.

Such processing is subject to the applicable service provider's privacy practices and relevant legal requirements.

When this Privacy Policy is updated, the "Last updated" date shown at the top of the page will be revised.
