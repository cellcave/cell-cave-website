Status Downloader: Video Saver

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Cell Cave handles information when you use Status Downloader: Video Saver.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

1. Overview

Status Downloader: Video Saver is provided by Cell Cave. We respect user privacy and aim to keep the app simple and transparent.

The app does not require users to create an account or sign in to use its main features.

2. Information We Do Not Directly Collect

Cell Cave does not directly ask users to provide their name, phone number, email address, or precise location when using the main features of Status Downloader: Video Saver.

3. Photos, Videos and Media

Status Downloader: Video Saver may request access to photos, videos, media, or device storage when this access is required for the app's core functionality.

Supported status content and media handled through the app are processed locally on the user's device as part of the app's normal functionality.

Cell Cave does not upload this locally handled media to Cell Cave servers as part of the app's normal status-saving functionality.

4. Device and Technical Information

Third-party services used in the app may automatically process certain technical information. Depending on the service, this may include:

  • Device and app information
  • Operating system information
  • App usage information
  • Advertising or app identifiers
  • IP address and network information
  • Ad interactions
  • Diagnostics and performance information
  • Approximate location that may be inferred from IP address

5. Analytics

Status Downloader: Video Saver uses Firebase Analytics to help understand app usage and improve the app experience.

Firebase Analytics may be used for both free and paid users.

6. Advertising

The free version of Status Downloader: Video Saver may display advertising.

Advertising services that may be used in the app include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Meta Audience Network
  • Mintegral
  • Pangle
  • Liftoff

These third-party advertising providers may process technical information necessary to provide, measure, personalize, limit, or protect advertising depending on user consent, device settings, region, and applicable law.

7. Paid Subscription

Status Downloader: Video Saver may offer a paid subscription through Google Play.

Paid subscribers do not receive advertising in the app.

Subscription payments are processed by Google Play. Cell Cave does not directly collect or process the user's full payment card details.

8. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services that operate under their own privacy policies.

Advertising and privacy choices may also be available through device settings, consent controls, and third-party service settings where applicable.
