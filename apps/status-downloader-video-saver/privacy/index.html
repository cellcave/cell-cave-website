Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Cell Cave handles information when you use Status Downloader: Video Saver.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

1. Overview

Status Downloader: Video Saver is provided by Cell Cave. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

The app does not require users to create an account or sign in to use its main features.

2. Information We Do Not Directly Collect

Cell Cave does not directly ask users to provide their name, email address, phone number, or precise location to use the main functionality of Status Downloader: Video Saver.

3. Photos, Videos, Media and Storage

Status Downloader: Video Saver may request access to photos, videos, media, or device storage when this access is necessary to provide the app's core functionality.

Supported status content and media handled through the app are processed locally on the user's device.

Cell Cave does not upload this locally handled status content or media to Cell Cave servers as part of the app's normal status-saving functionality.
